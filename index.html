Travel should not require an exhausting 17-tab browser window just to figure out which airport connection is manageable, which hotel is quiet, or which route keeps your energy intact. A flight is never just a flight — it is the connection, the airport, the hotel noise, the station layout, and what happens if plans change. Standard booking sites do not account for sensory overload, time-blindness, or the mental load of making a trip feel manageable.

I handle the complicated parts of the journey so you don't have to keep solving the same travel problems in real time.
What I Handle

The planning work that makes travel feel manageable again

Instead of sending you down a rabbit hole of hotel comparisons, layover tradeoffs, and station confusion, I handle the parts that are most stressful and least predictable.

Flights, trains, and connection strategy

I choose routes around your actual needs — not just the cheapest fare. That means better layovers, quieter airports, manageable transfers, and less decision fatigue.

Quiet, practical accommodations

I look for rooms away from elevators, ice machines, noisy traffic, and overstimulating layouts. I also request specific room details before you arrive so the stay starts calmer.

Airport and station walkthroughs

You receive clear, visual instructions for what to do, where to go, and what to expect at the exact airport or station you're using so there are fewer surprises on travel day.

Backup plans when travel shifts

If a delay, cancellation, or gate change happens, I monitor and reroute quickly so you are not standing in line at customer service while overwhelmed and exhausted.
