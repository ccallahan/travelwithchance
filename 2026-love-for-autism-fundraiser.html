2026 Love for Autism Fundraiser

By Chance Callahan  ·  September 27, 2026  ·  Events

Quick summary

The Travel with Chance team was at Love for Autism, presented by Ryker’s Foundation for Autism Awareness. Next up: Plant-O-Ween in Kechi on October 10.

Yesterday, the Travel with Chance team was at the Love for Autism event here in Wichita. We had an amazing time meeting families, providers, and talking with them about our services and how we can help the community.

Where to find us next: Plant-O-Ween

We look forward to seeing the community again at Valley Floral and Plant People of Wichita’s Plant-O-Ween pop-up:

  • When: Saturday, October 10, 10 AM to 2 PM
  • Where: 6200 N Oliver, Kechi, KS
  • What to expect: plant vendors, a food truck, and a giveaway table
