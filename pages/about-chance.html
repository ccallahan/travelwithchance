About Chance

I grew up here in Wichita, but spent the past decade living all over the country. During that time, I discovered one of my true passions is traveling and exploring new places. It's always incredible to see new faces and try new things.

Now that I'm settled back in town, I want to take my experiences and put them to good use, so I can help enable others to have amazing memories made as I did.

Why neurodivergent travel

I'm autistic myself. I know how much a loud hotel room, a rushed connection, or an unfamiliar station can take out of a trip, because I have dealt with all of them. Travel with Chance exists so that those details are settled before you leave home.

I'm a Fora Certified travel advisor, which means I can book flights, hotels, rail, cruises, and tours on your behalf and work with them directly on accommodations.

Outside of travel

I'm a huge nerd. I think Star Wars and Star Trek are both awesome, and I enjoy cooking and working on my skills with amateur radio.
